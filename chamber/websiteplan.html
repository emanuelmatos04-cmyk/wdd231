Santo Domingo Cultural Hub - Site Plan

Site Name

Santo Domingo Cultural Hub

This name was selected because it reflects the purpose of the site: to serve as a hub for events, attractions, and community activities that highlight the vibrant identity of Santo Domingo.

Optional domain availability: sdculturalhub.org

Site Purpose

The site provides a digital hub for Santo Domingo’s cultural life by listing events, attractions, and community activities. It also includes a form for visitors to express interest in attending events, making it interactive and user-focused.

Scenarios

  • What cultural events are happening in Santo Domingo this month?
  • How can I register my interest in attending a local festival?

Color Scheme

Main colors:

  • Orange (#e67300) → used for accents, buttons, and highlights.
  • Deep Blue (#004080) → used for headings and navigation elements.
  • Light background (#fdfdfd) → used for page background to ensure readability.

Typography

  • Georgia, serif → used for headings to give a formal, cultural tone.
  • Arial, sans-serif → used for body text for clarity and readability.
